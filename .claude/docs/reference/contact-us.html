Questions before you write in

How quickly will I get a reply?

Enquiries that reach us during office hours (Mon–Sat, 10:00–19:00 IST) are usually answered within four working hours. Anything that arrives overnight is answered the next working morning. A full day-by-day itinerary takes up to 24 working hours because we check ferry and hotel availability before quoting.

What is the fastest way to reach you?

WhatsApp. It reaches the same planners as the phone line but keeps the whole conversation — dates, hotel names, ferry timings — in one thread you can scroll back through while you travel.

Can I visit your office?

Yes. Our head office is in Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair) and we have a branch office in Karol Bagh, New Delhi. Both are open Mon–Sat, 10:00–19:00. Call ahead if you want a specific planner, as our island team is often out on the road.

I have already booked. Who do I contact during the trip?

Your booking confirmation carries a coordinator's direct number. That coordinator is on the islands for the whole of your trip and is reachable on WhatsApp — not a mainland call centre.

Do you charge for planning or quotes?

No. Itineraries, quotes and replanning are free. You pay only when you confirm a trip, and the first payment is a 25% advance that holds hotels and ferry seats.

Discover Andaman — 5 Nights / 6 Days

Port Blair, Havelock and Neil at a pace that leaves two unhurried beach days and a buffer if a ferry is cancelled — hotels, private cabs, ferry tickets and sightseeing included.

About this itinerary

Five nights is the shortest trip that covers Port Blair, Havelock and Neil without spending every second day on a ferry. This is the itinerary we sell most, and the one we recommend when someone is visiting the islands for the first time.

The route is built around the ferry timetable rather than a sightseeing checklist: two nights on Havelock so Radhanagar is not squeezed into an arrival afternoon, one night on Neil, and the return leg to Port Blair kept in the morning so a cancelled sailing still leaves you a day of margin before your flight. Prices are per person on twin sharing and exclude flights.

Day-by-day itinerary

Every day below can be moved, shortened or swapped. Replanning is free — you only pay the difference in what you change.

Day 1 · Port BlairDay 2 · HavelockDay 3 · HavelockDay 4 · Neil IslandDay 5 · Port BlairDay 6 · Departure
  1. Day 1Port BlairArrival day

    Arrive at Sri Vijaya Puram, Corbyn’s Cove and the light-and-sound show

    You are met at Veer Savarkar airport and driven to the hotel — about 15 minutes. The afternoon is deliberately light because most flights land tired: a drive along Corbyn’s Cove, then the Cellular Jail in time for the evening light-and-sound show, which is the one thing worth planning your first evening around.

    • Airport pick-up in a private air-conditioned cab
    • Corbyn’s Cove beach drive and free time
    • Cellular Jail National Memorial and the light-and-sound show (English show subject to schedule)
    Stay: Port BlairPrivate cabApprox. 4 hrs sightseeing
  2. Day 2HavelockSwaraj Dweep

    Catamaran to Havelock and sunset at Radhanagar Beach

    A morning catamaran gets you to Havelock in about 90 minutes, which leaves the whole day on the island. After check-in the afternoon is kept free for Radhanagar — Beach No. 7 — where the sunset is the reason people book this trip in the first place.

    • Morning private ferry, Port Blair to Havelock (seats held at booking)
    • Hotel check-in and time to settle
    • Radhanagar Beach for the sunset, with a lifeguard-marked swimming stretch
    Ferry: 90 minStay: HavelockBreakfast included
  3. Day 3HavelockFull day

    Elephant Beach by boat, and an afternoon that is yours

    A short speedboat ride from Havelock jetty reaches Elephant Beach, the calmest water on the island and where every water-sport operator is based. We keep the afternoon unscheduled on purpose — most guests either dive, or do nothing at all on Vijaynagar Beach.

    • Speedboat transfer to Elephant Beach and back
    • Optional snorkelling, sea walk, jet ski or a scuba try-dive (booked on the spot or in advance)
    • Afternoon free — Vijaynagar Beach (No. 5) is a 10-minute drive
    Stay: HavelockBreakfast includedAdd-ons priced below
  4. Day 4Neil IslandShaheed Dweep

    Ferry to Neil, Bharatpur and the Natural Bridge at low tide

    Neil is 40 minutes by ferry and a different pace entirely — smaller, flatter, easier to cycle. The Natural Bridge at Laxmanpur is only reachable around low tide, so the day is built around the tide table rather than the clock.

    • Ferry from Havelock to Neil
    • Bharatpur Beach — glass-bottom boat over the coral shelf
    • Laxmanpur Beach and the Natural Bridge (timed to low tide)
    Ferry: 40 minStay: Neil IslandBreakfast included
  5. Day 5Port BlairReturn leg

    Back to Port Blair, then Ross Island and North Bay

    The morning ferry returns you to Port Blair with the afternoon free for the two harbour islands: Ross Island — Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Dweep — with its colonial ruins and deer, and North Bay for the glass-bottom boat and coral viewing.

    • Ferry from Neil to Port Blair
    • Ross Island (Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Dweep) — ruins, deer and the audio walk
    • North Bay Island — glass-bottom boat, snorkelling on request
    Ferry: 2 hrsStay: Port BlairBreakfast included
  6. Day 6DepartureFly out

    Chidiya Tapu or the Sagarika market, then airport drop

    Depending on your flight time we fit in either Chidiya Tapu — the sunset point 25 km south, best on a late-afternoon departure — or the Sagarika government emporium for shell and wood work. Airport drop is timed for two hours before departure.

    • Breakfast and check-out
    • Chidiya Tapu or Sagarika market, flight timing permitting
    • Private cab drop at Veer Savarkar airport
    Airport dropBreakfast included

Good to know before you book

The ferries decide this itinerary

Private catamarans open bookings 60–90 days out and the morning slots go first. All three legs on this trip are held the moment your booking is confirmed. If a sailing is cancelled on a weather call — it happens between June and September — we move the day and absorb the rebooking, but the night you lose on an island is not recoverable inside a 5-night trip.

What the days actually feel like

Two of the six days are travel days with sightseeing bolted on, and three are unhurried. If you would rather not move hotels three times, the alternative is dropping Neil and taking a 4N/5D Port Blair–Havelock trip instead — say the word and we will quote it.

Questions about this package

Which ferry class is included in this package?

Private catamaran seats (Makruzz, Green Ocean or Nautika, whichever is sailing on your date) in the economy or base class, held at the time your trip is confirmed. Premium and luxury classes can be added — the difference is quoted upfront, usually ₹400–₹900 per person per leg.

Can I swap Neil Island for Baratang?

Yes, and it is a common change. Baratang needs an early convoy departure from Port Blair and a full day, so we usually swap it in for the Neil night and add a night in Port Blair instead. There is no replanning charge, only the price difference.

Is scuba diving included on Day 3?

No — water sports are excluded so you are not charged for something you may not do. A try-dive at Elephant Beach or Nemo Reef runs ₹3,500–₹4,500, sea walk ₹3,000 and snorkelling about ₹1,000. Tell us in advance and we will book slots; otherwise you can decide on the beach.

Is this itinerary comfortable with parents or young children?

Yes, with one change we suggest: keep the Elephant Beach boat but skip the speedboat return in choppy weather, and take the glass-bottom boat at North Bay instead of snorkelling. The ferry legs are the only fixed early starts, and both are mid-morning.

What is the best time to do this trip?

October to May. December and January have the calmest water and the highest prices; February and March are the sweet spot. June to September is the monsoon — ferries get cancelled on weather calls, so we only run this itinerary then with a buffer night built in.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to three months for December and January, about a month for the rest of the season. On this itinerary the constraint is almost always the Havelock hotel and the morning ferry slot, not flights.

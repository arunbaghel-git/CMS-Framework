    FAQ

    The questions we get asked most

    Can we customise our tour package?

    Yes, and most people do. Every package on this page is a starting point — swap an island, change the resort category, add or drop a night, build in a dive course. We rebuild the itinerary and re-quote as many times as you need, at no charge. Roughly seven in ten trips we run end up different from the package they started as.

    How many days should I plan for an Andaman trip?

    Five nights is the practical minimum for Port Blair plus Havelock. Six or seven nights lets you add Neil Island without spending your holiday on ferries. Under four nights, most of the trip is transit. If you want Baratang, Rangat or Diglipur in North and Middle Andaman, plan on eight nights or more.

    Are you actually based in the Andaman Islands?

    Yes. Our head office is at Hotel Classic Regency, AIR Road, Delanipur, Sri Vijaya Puram, and our operations team, vehicles and boats are on the islands. We also keep a Delhi branch for mainland enquiries. It matters when a ferry gets cancelled at 6am — someone is 20 minutes away rather than 2,000 kilometres.

    How expensive is a trip to the Andamans?

    Budget trips start around ₹6,900 per person for a short stay on twin sharing; a typical 5N/6D package with 3-star hotels, ferries and sightseeing runs ₹25,000–₹38,000 per person; luxury beachfront itineraries go past ₹55,000. Flights to Port Blair are quoted separately so you can book them yourself. Every quote we send is itemised — accommodation, ferry class, transfers, permits and activities are listed individually.

    Do I need a passport, visa or permit?

    Indian nationals need no passport — Aadhaar, a driving licence or a Voter ID is enough. Foreign nationals need a valid Indian visa and passport, checked at Port Blair immigration on arrival. Some islands additionally require a Restricted Area Permit, which we arrange as part of your package.

    What happens if a ferry or activity is cancelled?

    Cancellations by the administration for weather are common in shoulder season. If a ferry or an activity you have paid for is called off, we either re-slot it later in your trip or refund that component in full. Your planner handles the rebooking; you do not queue at a counter.

    When is the best time to visit?

    Mid-October to mid-May gives the driest weather and calmest seas. December and January are peak — book two to three months ahead. June to August is monsoon: prices drop sharply and the islands are at their greenest, but expect cancelled ferries and closed water sports on rough days.

Havelock Island

Swaraj Dweep · 2 hrs by ferry from Port Blair

Home to Radhanagar Beach, regularly rated among Asia's best, plus Elephant Beach and the archipelago's most established dive schools. Most first trips are anchored here for three or four nights.

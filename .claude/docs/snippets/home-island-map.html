Havelock Island

Swaraj Dweep · 90 min by catamaran from Port Blair

Home to Radhanagar Beach, regularly rated among Asia’s best, plus Elephant Beach and the archipelago’s most established dive schools. Most first trips are anchored here for three or four nights.

Don't miss
  • Radhanagar Beach
  • Elephant Beach snorkelling
  • Scuba at Nemo Reef
  • Kalapathar sunrise

Port Blair

Sri Vijaya Puram · Veer Savarkar Airport

Your arrival point and the administrative capital. Cellular Jail and its light-and-sound show, Ross Island, Corbyn’s Cove and the museums are all here. Most itineraries start and end with one or two nights in Port Blair.

Don't miss
  • Cellular Jail light & sound
  • Ross Island
  • Corbyn’s Cove
  • Samudrika Museum

Neil Island

Shaheed Dweep · 2 hrs from Port Blair, 60 min from Havelock

Smaller, greener and far quieter than Havelock. Bharatpur for snorkelling, Laxmanpur for sunset and the natural rock bridge at low tide. Two nights is usually enough.

Don't miss
  • Bharatpur Beach
  • Natural rock bridge
  • Laxmanpur sunset
  • Glass-bottom boat

Baratang Island

Middle Andaman · 3.5 hrs by road convoy from Port Blair

Reached through the Jarawa reserve on a timed convoy, then a mangrove creek boat to the limestone caves. Mud volcanoes and Parrot Island roosting at dusk. Usually done as a long day trip.

Don't miss
  • Limestone caves
  • Mud volcanoes
  • Mangrove creek ride
  • Parrot Island at dusk

Diglipur

North Andaman · 12 hrs by road or overnight ferry

The far north. Ross and Smith, two islands joined by a natural sandbar, plus Saddle Peak, turtle nesting at Kalipur and the Alfred Caves. Worth it only if you have eight nights or more.

Don't miss
  • Ross & Smith sandbar
  • Saddle Peak trek
  • Turtle nesting, Kalipur
  • Alfred Caves

Rangat

Middle Andaman · 6 hrs by road from Port Blair

Mostly a stopover on the way to Diglipur, but the Dhaninallah mangrove walkway and turtle nesting at Cuthbert Bay are genuinely worth the stop. Amkunj Beach for a swim.

Don't miss
  • Dhaninallah mangrove walk
  • Cuthbert Bay turtles
  • Amkunj Beach
  • Panchavati waterfall

Long Island

Middle Andaman · boat from Rangat or Havelock

No cars, a single village, and Lalaji Bay reached by boat or a two-hour jungle walk. For travellers who want the islands without any infrastructure at all.

Don't miss
  • Lalaji Bay Beach
  • Jungle trail walk
  • Merk Bay day boat
  • No-car village life

Little Andaman

South · 8 hrs by government ferry from Port Blair

The surfing island. Butler Bay, White Surf and Whisper Wave waterfalls, and almost no other tourists. Ferry-dependent and not for tight schedules.

Don't miss
  • Butler Bay surfing
  • White Surf waterfall
  • Whisper Wave falls
  • Netaji Nagar Beach

Barren Island

India’s only active volcano · liveaboard or day charter

You cannot land here. Boats circle the caldera and the dive sites around it are the most dramatic in the country — black volcanic sand, manta rays and steep walls. Charter only.

Don't miss
  • Volcano caldera cruise
  • Manta ray dive sites
  • Black sand walls
  • Liveaboard charters
